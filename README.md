## What customers get

### A PIM that supports the business

Product data should not become a bottleneck every time you add a market, channel, product line, or business unit. We design models that handle real complexity: inheritance, variants, taxonomies, workflows, relationships, and quality rules.

### A data foundation people can trust

Pimcore can become the single source of truth for product and master data, but only when the model, governance, and integrations agree. We connect ERP, PLM, commerce, middleware, portals, Web2Print, and downstream channels around a coherent data foundation.

### A migration with a reason behind it

Migration is not a technology swap. It is a business decision about data ownership, operating cost, flexibility, and risk. We assess the starting point, translate the existing model, plan the transition, and make the cutover measurable.

### AI that fits the process

AI is useful when it solves a defined business task. It is not useful when it is added as a layer of uncertainty.

We design specialised AI agents and agentic workflows with:

- a bounded task and a named process owner
- only the access required for that task
- fixed workflow steps wherever deterministic automation is enough
- validation and human approval at the points that need judgement
- clear handover, logging, and fallback paths

If conventional automation is the simpler answer, we will say so.
